# Philosophy

- **Publisher-first** — metrics and inventory stay on the operator's infrastructure.
- **Measurable by contract** — impression and click definitions are versioned in docs, not implied.
- **Safe creatives** — reviewed bundles beat arbitrary ad tags for self-hosted trust.
- **Global UI** — six locales from day one of the admin/embed surface (ADR 0011).
- **Money at the edge** — Serve tracks budgets; Invoice keeps tax and payment SSOT.
